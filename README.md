# Data Privacy Practicals

### B.Sc. (Hons.) Computer Science | Data Privacy

This repository contains practical assignments completed as part of the Data Privacy course. It includes website-based privacy audits, privacy impact assessments, cryptography implementations, anonymization techniques, regulatory compliance analysis, and privacy-related case studies.

## Objectives

* Understand the principles of data privacy and protection.
* Analyze privacy practices of real-world websites and platforms.
* Identify potential privacy risks and recommend mitigation strategies.
* Explore cryptographic and privacy-enhancing technologies.
* Understand data protection regulations and ethical considerations.
